# C54

## Statement

Let M be a simple 3-polytope (a simple 3-connected planar map), with p_k the number of k-gonal faces, f_2 the total number of faces, and sum_{k>=7} p_k the number of faces of size at least 7. Suppose f_2 >= 54, p_4 <= 3. Then 2*p_6 >= -p_5 - sum_pk_after_p6 + 19.

**Formula.** `if ((is_simple) and (f_2>=_54) and (p_4 <= 3)), then p6 >= ((((-0.5 * p5) + (-0.5 * sum_pk_after_p6)) + 9.5))`
