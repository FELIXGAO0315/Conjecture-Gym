## Statement

Let M be a simple 3-polytope (a simple 3-connected planar map), with p_k the number of k-gonal faces, f_2 the total number of faces, and sum_{k>=7} p_k the number of faces of size at least 7. Suppose f_2 >= 8, p_5 = 0, sum_pk_after_p6 <= 3. Then 2*p_6 >= -p_4 + 2*p_5 + 4.

**Formula.** `if ((is_simple) and (f_2>=_8) and (p_5 = 0) and (sum_pk_after_p6 <= 3)), then p6 >= ((((-0.5 * p4) + p5) + 2))`
