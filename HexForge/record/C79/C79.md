## Statement

Let M be a simple 3-polytope (a simple 3-connected planar map), with p_k the number of k-gonal faces, f_2 the total number of faces, and sum_{k>=7} p_k the number of faces of size at least 7. Suppose f_2 >= 59, p_5 = 0, p_4 >= 2. Then 2*p_6 >= -2*p_4 - sum_pk_after_p6 + 25.

**Formula.** `if ((is_simple) and (f_2>=_59) and (p_5 = 0) and (p_4 >= 2)), then p6 >= ((((-1 * p4) + (-0.5 * sum_pk_after_p6)) + 12.5))`
