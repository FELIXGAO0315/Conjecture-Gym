## Statement

Let M be a simple 3-polytope (a simple 3-connected planar map), with p_k the number of k-gonal faces, f_2 the total number of faces, and sum_{k>=7} p_k the number of faces of size at least 7. Suppose f_2 >= 15, 2*p_3 - 3*p_4 >= 0. Then p_6 >= -p_5 - sum_pk_after_p6 + 5.

**Formula.** `if ((is_simple) and (f_2>=_15) and (2*p_3 - 3*p_4 >= 0)), then p6 >= ((((-1 * p5) + (-1 * sum_pk_after_p6)) + 5))`
