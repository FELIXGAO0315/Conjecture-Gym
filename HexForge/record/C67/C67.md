## Statement

Let M be a simple 3-polytope (a simple 3-connected planar map), with p_k the number of k-gonal faces, f_2 the total number of faces, and sum_{k>=7} p_k the number of faces of size at least 7. Suppose 3*p_4 - 2*p_5 >= 0, -3*p_3 + p_4 >= 0, f_2 >= 43. Then p_6 >= -2*p_4 + 16.

**Formula.** `if ((is_simple) and (3*p_4 - 2*p_5 >= 0) and (-3*p_3 + p_4 >= 0) and (f_2>=_43)), then p6 >= (((-2 * p4) + 16))`
