# C5

## Statement

Let M be a simple 3-polytope (a simple 3-connected planar map), with p_k the number of k-gonal faces, f_2 the total number of faces, and sum_{k>=7} p_k the number of faces of size at least 7. Suppose f_2 >= 15. Then p_6 >= (sum_{k>=11} p_k) - f_2 + 13.

**Formula.** `if ((is_simple) and (f_2 >= 15)), then (p_6 >= (sum_{k>=11} p_k) - f_2 + 13)`

    - Inventory `nonneg_p_9`: `1*p_9 >= 0`.
