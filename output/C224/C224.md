## Statement

Let G be a finite simple graph with the usual invariants (n, m, max_deg, min_deg, chi, omega, alpha, tau, diam, radius, girth). Suppose has_cycle, girth <= 5. Then 2*girth <= 3*chi + 3*alpha - 4.

**Formula.** `if ((has_cycle) and (girth <= 5)), then (2*girth <= 3*chi + 3*alpha - 4)`
