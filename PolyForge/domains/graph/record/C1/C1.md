## Statement

Let G be a finite simple graph with the usual invariants (n, m, max_deg, min_deg, chi, omega, alpha, tau, diam, radius, girth). Suppose omega >= 2*max_deg, min_deg <= 1. Then 2*max_deg <= chi + omega - 2.

**Formula.** `if ((omega >= 2*max_deg) and (min_deg <= 1)), then (2*max_deg <= chi + omega - 2)`
