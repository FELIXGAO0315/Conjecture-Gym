## Statement

Let G be a finite simple graph with the usual invariants (n, m, max_deg, min_deg, chi, omega, alpha, tau, diam, radius, girth). Suppose tau >= 3. Then n >= -3*chi - 2*alpha + 18.

**Formula.** `if ((tau >= 3)), then (n >= -3*chi - 2*alpha + 18)`
