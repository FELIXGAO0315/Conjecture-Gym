## Statement

Let G be a finite simple graph with the usual invariants (n, m, max_deg, min_deg, chi, omega, alpha, tau, diam, radius, girth). Suppose is_tree, alpha >= 3. Then min_deg <= 3*alpha - 8.

**Formula.** `if ((is_tree) and (alpha >= 3)), then (min_deg <= 3*alpha - 8)`
